Pancit Canton

Back to Homepage

Description

Pancit canton is a stir-fried Filipino noodle dish loaded with meat, shrimp, and vegetables. Noodles are a must at Filipino birthdays because they symbolize long life. It's quick to cook and perfect for sharing.

Ingredients

  • 250 grams pancit canton noodles
  • 200 grams pork, sliced into thin strips
  • 200 grams shrimp, peeled
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, cut into thin strips
  • 2 cups cabbage, shredded
  • 1 cup snow peas
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Calamansi, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a wok and sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  2. Add the pork and cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add the shrimp and cook until they turn pink.
  4. Add the carrot, snow peas, and cabbage. Stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  5. Pour in the chicken broth, soy sauce, and oyster sauce. Bring to a boil.
  6. Add the noodles and toss until they absorb most of the liquid.
  7. Serve hot with calamansi on the side.
