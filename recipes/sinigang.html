Sinigang na Baboy

Back to Homepage

Description

Sinigang is a sour and savory Filipino soup made with tamarind. This version uses pork ribs and fresh vegetables. It's the perfect comfort food on a rainy day.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork ribs, cut into pieces
  • 2 liters water
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 pack tamarind soup mix (about 40 grams)
  • 1 medium radish, sliced
  • 1 bunch string beans, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 2 eggplants, sliced
  • 2 long green chilies
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong)
  • Fish sauce, to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the pork ribs with the onion and tomatoes in a large pot of water.
  2. Skim off the scum that rises to the surface, then simmer for 45 to 60 minutes until the pork is tender.
  3. Stir in the tamarind soup mix.
  4. Add the radish and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the string beans, eggplants, and green chilies. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  6. Season with fish sauce to taste.
  7. Add the water spinach, then turn off the heat and cover the pot for 2 minutes.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice.
